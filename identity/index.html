Where your data goes

Two lists, both complete. If something is missing from the second list, it is a mistake worth telling us about.

Stays on your phone

  • Files you open, record, scan or transcribe. Transcription runs on the device with a local Whisper model, so audio is not uploaded.
  • Your playlists, IPTV sources, notes, tasks, flashcards and clipboard history.
  • App lock PINs, patterns and fingerprint settings.
  • Anything an app saves for later. There is no Tidox account and no cloud sync, so there is nowhere for it to go.

Leaves your phone

Ad requests Google AdMob
Free versions show ads. The ad request carries the usual advertising identifiers, which you can reset or limit in Android settings.
Crash reports Firebase Crashlytics
A stack trace and device model when an app crashes, so the bug can be found.
Usage analytics Firebase Analytics and PostHog
Which screens and features get used, so development time goes to the parts people actually open.
Session recordings PostHog
Anonymised playback of how a screen was used, with text masked. It shows where people get stuck.
Website visits Google Analytics
Page views on tidox.online. This is the website, not the apps.

Full detail, including how to contact us about your data, is in the privacy policy.

What that means for you

Your content is not the product

The files, recordings and notes you create in a Tidox app are never uploaded, sold or shared. What leaves the device is listed above, in full.

No account, no sign-in

No Tidox app asks you to register. There is no password to lose and no profile to delete, because none is created.

Paying removes the ads

Premium versions are a one-time purchase that switches the ads off. Nothing else changes, and the free version is not crippled to push the upgrade.

Uninstalling ends it

App data lives on the device. Removing the app removes it. Nothing is kept on a server waiting for you to come back.

When something breaks

Every app has Help & Support in its menu, which sends the report with the app version and device model already filled in. That is the fastest route, because it saves the back and forth about which build you are running.

Email works too. A real person reads it — Tidox is a small studio, not a support desk, so expect a human reply rather than an instant one.
