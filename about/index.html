The Story

Tidox started with a simple observation: most mobile apps collect far more data than they need. We wondered if it was possible to build powerful, useful apps that respected user privacy from the ground up.

The answer was yes. By processing everything on-device and never requiring cloud sync, we created apps that are both more private and more reliable. No internet connection? No problem. Concerned about data breaches? Your data never leaves your device.

Today, Tidox offers eighteen focused apps spanning media playback, audio intelligence, productivity, security, communication, and smart home control. Each app does one thing exceptionally well, without the bloat or privacy concerns of traditional alternatives.

What we stand for

01

Privacy is Non-Negotiable

We never collect personal data. No analytics, no tracking, no telemetry. Your usage patterns are yours alone.

02

Simplicity Over Features

We resist feature bloat. Every feature must earn its place by solving a real user problem without adding complexity.

03

Offline First

Internet connectivity should be optional, not required. All our apps work fully offline, giving you reliability and privacy.

04

Fair Pricing

Free versions have real value. Premium unlocks advanced features at a fair one-time price. No subscriptions, no upsells.
